**Time zone.** Crons are expressed in UTC (`scheduler.js:40`). "Today" is computed as
UTC + 5:30 by hand (`morningBriefEngine.js:133` `istDate`, `scheduler.js:110`). No code uses
`Asia/Kolkata` explicitly; the arithmetic is correct because IST has no DST.

**If the process is asleep at 02:00 UTC:** the `node-cron` job does not fire (Render free stops
the process after ~15 idle minutes). The brief then only goes out when something wakes the
server *after* 07:30 IST and the start-up catch-up or `/api/admin/wake` runs it.

**`.github/workflows/wake.yml`** calls `GET {BACKEND_URL}/api/admin/wake` with header
`x-admin-key` (curl, 180 s timeout, 4 retries). Schedule (UTC): `2 2 * * 1-5` (07:32 IST),
`32 3 * * *`, `35 4-9 * * 1-5`, `5 11 * * 1-5`, `35 12 * * 1-5`, `35 16 * * 1-5`,
`35 14 * * 0`. The workflow **does trigger the brief** (through catch-up), not just warm the server.

## Q4. How BUY/WATCH and target/stop are decided

- Composite of 9 levers → `BUY ≥ 70`, `WATCH 50–69`, `SELL < 50`; high conviction ≥ 75 (`instrumentEngine.js:419-424`).
- **No-data rule:** an instrument with no own price history is forced to WATCH (`leverEngine.js:430-440` `insufficient_data`; applied at `instrumentEngine.js:920-924`).
- Risk gate can veto a BUY to WATCH (`riskGateEngine.js`; result passed in as `gateResult`).
- Target/stop: fixed percentages (Q2). **There is no reward:risk check.** Ratios implied by the tables: debt/G-Sec 0.5% vs 3–4% stop (≈0.13–0.17); large-cap equity weekly 2% vs 5% (0.4); small-cap 4% vs 10% (0.4). Monthly horizons reach 1.2 at best. Every BUY the engine can emit has reward:risk < 1.5.

## Q5. Which asset classes can reach the brief, and why only debt appeared

`buildIdeas` (`morningBriefEngine.js:228-313`) takes fresh (≤10 days), risk-gate-passed, active
signals in the categories allowed for the client's risk band (`RISK_CATEGORY_MAP`,
`morningBriefEngine.js:35-40`), sorted by confidence.

Why only debt:
1. **Yahoo blocks history** → all 74 stocks/ETFs/gold ETFs score on category baselines and are forced to WATCH.
2. **Category baselines favour debt:** with only 1 of 9 levers measured, static bonds scored 71 and debt funds 52–70, against 57–61 for equities (`leverEngine.js` baselines). Sorted by confidence, debt wins.
3. **On 3 Nov the BUYs were 12 Sep G-Sec signals** that were still active; the 10-day freshness filter was added later, and static bonds could still be BUY before the no-data rule.
4. **Demo accounts have no risk score,** so every one uses the default "moderate" band.

Since PR #4: static instruments and unmeasured scores are excluded from ideas, so today only
measured mutual funds can appear — still debt-heavy because no equity is measured.
